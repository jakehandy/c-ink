Hardware

Until ENILINX publishes the TICKEY's pinout and panel driver, C-Ink learns them. Run tools/tickey-probe on the stock firmware to get a board profile, load it here and send it. Learn buttons finds the three buttons by watching the pins while you press them.

Privacy

C-Ink only lets Claude use read-only tools, seals every digest for your device with its own key, and never sends anything anywhere else.

Away mode

        
On your home or office Wi-Fi, the TICKEY fetches straight from this computer. To keep it syncing anywhere else, add a relay. It only ever stores encrypted blobs.

      

        

        

        
Deploy the tiny relay in relay/ to Cloudflare Workers (free tier is plenty), then press "Update settings only" on the Device page so the TICKEY learns the URL.

        

      

      

        
Offline behaviour

        
Each digest covers the next 12 hours. Away from any known Wi-Fi, the TICKEY keeps re-ranking it as meetings approach and expire, shows when it last synced, and backs off retries to save battery.
